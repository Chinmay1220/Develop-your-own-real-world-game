# Rewind: 2-minute demo video script

Record the screen at http://localhost:3000/rewind.html. Speak slowly and keep each part short.

**0:00–0:15. The hook (start screen on camera)**
> "World models predict the future. Rewind runs one backwards. Show it a photo of what happened, and Meta's AI works out how it happened, and paints the moments before."

**0:15–0:35. Give it a photo**
- Click **"Try the example: a car against a guardrail"**, or drop your own photo.
> "This is one photo: a car against a guardrail on a wet road. Muse Wonder, Meta's frontier model, is reading the evidence."

**0:35–1:05. The evidence and the explanations (when the case opens)**
- Point at the numbered pins, then the list on the right.
> "It found the evidence: curved tyre marks, where the damage came from, the wet road, how little debris there is."
- Point at the explanation cards and their likelihood bars.
> "Then it worked backwards with physics: about 60 to 80 kilometres an hour, wet grip around 0.3 to 0.4 g. It ranks every possibility, with the evidence for and against each one, and what could have prevented it."

**1:05–1:35. Rewind (the money shot)**
- Click **▶ Play**.
> "Now Muse Image paints the moments before, in the same real place, going backwards from the photo. Ten seconds before: the car coming round the bend. One second before: sliding toward the rail. And now: the photo."
- Click the second explanation to show another timeline being painted.

**1:35–1:55. Your own photo (optional, if you staged one)**
- Drop your own photo (for example a knocked-over glass) and show the explanations.

**1:55–2:00. The close**
> "Rewind: Muse Wonder investigates, Muse Image paints the past, Muse Spark keeps it safe. A world model, running backwards."

**Before recording:**
- Make sure the server is running and the example has been run once, so the page is warm.
- Use a 1440 × 900 or larger browser window.
